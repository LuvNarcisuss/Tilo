# Tilo Privacy Policy
Effective November 6, 2026

## Scope
This policy explains how Tilo handles information when you use its cooking, planning and social features. Tilo is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

## Information and purposes
We process your account identifier, login session and agreement choices to keep your account available and protect access. Your profile may include a display name, username, biography, chosen dietary preferences, cooking skill level and an optional photo.

We store the recipes you publish, likes, saved recipes, follows, meal plans, grocery lists, messages, group membership, drafts, reading progress, reports and blocks so these features work across visits. Dietary preferences are optional; do not include sensitive health details in a public profile. Public profile details and published recipes can be viewed by other signed-in users. Private messages are available to authorized conversation members.

## Photos, notifications and choices
You choose whether to select a photo for an avatar or recipe. Only selected media is uploaded; Tilo does not upload your photo library. Camera access is used only when you choose a supported capture action. Declining optional permissions does not prevent browsing recipes. Notification preferences do not guarantee delivery when a notification service is unavailable.

## Purchases when available
If you buy digital consumables, Tilo keeps an account-scoped transaction ledger on this device containing the transaction identifier, product, quantity and wallet changes. Apple handles payment and financial details. Tilo does not collect your card or bank account information. This local ledger does not promise cross-device recovery of a consumable balance.

## Storage and service providers
Tilo uses Supabase for account authentication, database records, media storage and realtime messaging. Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and access to private records is restricted by account and conversation membership. Publicly shared media and published legal documents are openly accessible. No security measure can guarantee complete protection. Operational diagnostics may be processed to maintain reliability and investigate abuse.

## Your controls
Edit profile information in Me, remove your own published recipes, manage saves and likes, and use report and block controls. Meal plans and grocery entries can be removed from their respective screens. Deleting a conversation from your inbox hides it for you; it does not remove another participant's copy. Group membership changes control access to future retrieval, but cannot erase content another member has already seen or copied.

Use Delete Account in Settings to remove your account and the account-owned information linked to it. Information may remain where required for legal obligations or in provider backups until the relevant backup cycle completes. We do not promise a fixed retention period that has not been established. A device-only anonymous account may be unrecoverable after logout, reinstalling or losing the device.

## Contact and changes
Contact: support@toli.com. This address is planned and its activation has not yet been confirmed. In-app controls remain available for account management. Updates to this policy show a new effective date in the app.
