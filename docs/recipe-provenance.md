# Recipe provenance

Twelve original English recipes were authored for Tilo on November 3, 2026. No third-party recipe prose was copied. Licensed real photographs were selected separately and are documented in media-inventory.md and Assets/SourceMedia/manifest.json. A photograph illustrates the named style of dish; it does not prove the recipe was cooked by its photographer.

The collection includes tomato basil spaghetti, berry pancakes, a cheeseburger with fries, Margherita pizza, lemon herb salmon, pepper steak, vegetable soup, garden salad, roast chicken, bruschetta, mozzarella toast and a chicken tray bake.

Instructions, ingredient quantities, servings, timings and cuisine tags are in catalog-recipes.json and the reproducible scripts/create_catalog.py. Timings are preparation estimates. No nutrition analysis was performed; zero-valued schema compatibility nutrition fields are not displayed as nutrient facts.

Meat, poultry and seafood thermometer guidance was checked against [FoodSafety.gov's safe minimum temperature chart](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures) on November 3, 2026: steak 63°C with three minutes of rest, ground beef 71°C, poultry 74°C and fish 63°C. Recipes cannot replace individual allergy assessment or ingredient-label checks.

Business recipe/media records are served from the dedicated Supabase project. Source JSON and original/download archives do not enter the iOS application target.
